For Meeting Requesters

How Influence Board works.

Eight steps from finding the right executive to a meeting that funds a worthy cause.

  1. 1

    Start on your dashboard

    Click Find Business Influencers, the blue button at the top of your dashboard. Every request you send shows up below it, sorted into Submitted, Accepted, Completed, and Declined.

  2. 2

    Find executives

    Search by name, or narrow the list with filters for business function, state, and meeting value (the donation amount).

  3. 3

    Check the fit before you request

    Each profile lists the topics the executive is open to discussing and the topics the executive is not interested in. Read both before you do anything else.

    Send a request only when both are true

    • ✓  Your offer fits one of the Approved Conversation Topics.
    • ✓  Your offer is not on the Not Interested In list.

    If either one doesn't fit, move on to another executive.

  4. 4

    Write your pitch

    Click Compose Request. Write up to 300 words that speak to one of the approved topics, and add a video or files if you like.

    What makes a pitch land

    • Personalized. Mention their company, a recent initiative, or their chosen cause.
    • Value-led. Say what you bring and why it matters to them.
    • Human. Write like a person. No jargon or filler.
    • Focused. Under 300 words.
    • Grateful. End with thanks and an easy next step.
    See a sample pitch ›
  5. 5

    Agree and submit

    Check the box that says "If my request is accepted, I agree to pay." Then click Submit Request. You pay nothing unless the executive accepts.

After you submit

We follow up for you

Our system emails the executive, and our team calls and reaches out to get a response. A lot happens behind the scenes.

Not every request gets an answer

About 30% of requests are accepted. Much of it comes down to the executive's schedule. If a request goes unanswered, we withdraw it, and you can resubmit later.

Open requests are limited

You can only have a set number of requests out at once, and we watch for spam. Send requests only to executives who fit, so the platform works for everyone.

  1. 6

    The executive accepts

    The executive reviews your request. If it fits, the executive clicks Accept and picks the charity that receives the donation. You are notified on your dashboard when the executive accepts or declines.

  2. 7

    Pay for the meeting

    If the executive accepts, click Pay Now on your dashboard. You pay the donation plus a 30% platform fee. Full refund if the meeting doesn't happen.

  3. 8

    Schedule and meet

    Once you pay, the executive's contact info appears. Schedule the meeting, then click Mark Scheduled and Mark Completed. Influence Board sends the donation to the cause.

What it costs

Each executive sets a requested donation. Most set between $250 and $500. You pay that donation plus a 30% platform fee, and only after the executive accepts.

How it works

You set the terms and we handle the enforcing.

1

Set your terms

Tell us the topics you'd want to meet about, the topics you'd like restricted, how often requests can reach you each month, and choose the causes your meetings will support.

2

Requests are screened

Every request that comes through the platform gets checked against your terms before you ever see it. The ones that miss are turned away. The ones that match include context with the donation already attached if you accept.

3

You're in control

Accept it or decline it, with no obligation either way. Many executives end up routing all of their vendor outreach through the platform.

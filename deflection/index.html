Send vendor emails to Influence Board with one drag

Set this up once in Microsoft Power Automate. Then drag any vendor email into a folder, and a reply goes out from your inbox pointing the vendor to Influence Board.

  1. 1Vendor emails you
  2. 2You drag it to
    Vendors Forward
  3. 3Reply goes out
    within 5 min

1Make a folder in Outlook

Create a new Outlook folder named Vendors Forward. If another tool already uses a folder called "Vendors," keep this one separate.

2Build the flow

  1. Go to make.powerautomate.com and click Create in the left menu.
  1. Click Automated cloud flow and name it Vendors Forward, same as the folder.
  2. Search new email arrives, select When a new email arrives (V3) under Office 365 Outlook, then click Create.
  1. Click the When a new email arrives (V3) box. Next to Advanced parameters, click Show all.
  2. Set Folder to Vendors Forward with the folder icon. Leave the rest as is.
Says "Not connected"? Click Change connection at the bottom of the panel and pick your work account. If it isn't listed, click Add new and sign in.

3Add the reply and save

  1. Click the + under the first box, then Add an action. Search Reply to email and pick Reply to email (V3).
  2. Message Id: click in the field, then the lightning bolt (or type /). Under When a new email arrives, choose Message Id.
  3. Open Advanced parameters and check Body and Reply All.
  4. Body: paste the message below. Adjust as desired and add your name.
Templated, adjust as desired

Thanks for reaching out to me.

To make the best use of your time and mine, all outreach is routed through Influence Board. Visit the link to learn how the process works and connect with the right person on our team.

[Your name]

  1. Highlight Influence Board, click the link icon in the toolbar, and paste:
    https://influenceboard.com/new-model-for-executive-access/
  2. Reply All: set to No. Leave To, CC, BCC and Subject blank. The reply goes to the vendor and keeps their subject line.
  3. Click Save at the top. A green bar says "Your flow is ready to go."

4Test it

  1. From a personal email, send a short test to your work email.
  2. In Outlook, drag it into Vendors Forward.
  3. Within 5 minutes, the reply lands in your personal inbox. Check spam, and click the link to make sure it works.

To see runs, go to My flows and open Vendors Forward. Status should be On.

If something's off

"Not connected" at the bottom of a boxClick Change connection and pick your work account.
Red "Invalid parameters" on the reply boxMessage Id is empty. Add the Message Id tag from step 3.
No reply after 5 minutesSave the flow before dragging an email in. Emails moved before saving won't trigger it. Send a fresh test.
An older email didn't get a replyMicrosoft goes by when an email first arrived, so emails that are days old can get skipped. Use it on new vendor emails as they come in.
Can't open Power AutomateYour Microsoft 365 plan may not include it. Check with your IT team.

FAQs

Is this pay-to-play?

No, you can't buy a yes. Every request is checked against the terms the executive set, and then they decide whether they will take the meeting. You're only charged if they accept.

What if they cancel?

We'll try our best to get them to reschedule. If the meeting still doesn't happen, you don't pay for it, and any charge will be refunded.

What does it cost?

We've got different services depending on your company's offering and needs. The cost per meeting depends on the executive. Each one sets a donation for their time.

Where does the money go?

100% of your donation goes to the executive's chosen charity. Influence Board's platform fee is charged separately, so it never reduces the amount the charity receives.

Can I pick the executive?

Yes. Name who you want to meet, and if they're on Influence Board, we'll submit your request to them. If they're not, or you're not sure yet, tell us who you sell to and we'll match you with executives who fit, and we can recruit the others to join as part of the service.

Do I need to sign a contract?

A short agreement, yes. Some options have no monthly fee and no commitment. Others run on a set term. We'll recommend the one that fits after you introduce yourself.
